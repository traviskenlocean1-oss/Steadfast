Game Ready: Field to Pro

A speed, power and conditioning program for athletes chasing the next level, built around the season of Jalen Price.

For: High School & College Athletes

The Goal

What We Built

  • Strategy

  • Recovery
  • Nutrition
  • Recovery

Local high school and college athletes needed a program to prepare rising athletes like Jalen Price as they made the leap from high school ball to college football. The goal was to build a growth plan that could carry the next generation of athletes.

It couldn’t be just another summer camp. The program had to balance raw speed and power with the deeper work of growth and staying healthy. The challenge was to honor every athlete’s own season while delivering something serious enough for coaches and scouts to stand behind. This wasn’t about making training look cool. Training already is cool. It was about building the nerves, the grit, and the heart of a kid chasing the next level.

Approach

Most people think of athlete training as a few sprints after practice or maybe a summer combine camp. The truth is, real sports performance is demanding. It’s where future college and pro athletes build their base. Families pour in money, time, and weekends, and the competition is ruthless. We wanted to train athletes in that proper light: gritty, high-stakes, and real, while also giving them a look inside their own numbers. On the field, we train like big-league programs, making local athletes feel like they belong on ESPN. In the gym, we get more detailed, working on the nerves, focus, and recovery that you can’t see from the outside.

At the end of the day, it isn’t about flashy drills or trick workouts, although we certainly add plenty of fun ones... It’s about preparing athletes for the pressure they’re under and the dream they’re chasing.

How it works

  • FIELD ROOTS

    We train on a real turf field, where Sam and Jalen had run sprints side by side on weekends. That connection gives us a deep understanding of how to plan speed work in a way that feels true to the sport.

  • TWO WORLDS

    To build both the speed of game day and the strength under it, we built two training worlds. On the field, sessions lean fast, with sprints, cuts and game-day energy. In the gym, heavy lifts and jump work build a powerful, explosive base. The back-and-forth gives athletes both the physical and mental sides of chasing the next level.

  • TESTING TOOLS

    Every testing tool is chosen to take athlete training beyond the guesswork people think it is. Timing gates give every sprint an honest number down to the hundredth. A force plate adds a real look at jump power. Velocity trackers on the bar show how fast each rep moves at every weight. And film review captures the kind of small details only coaches and athletes obsess over. Together, these tools turn training into something precise and personal at the same time.
